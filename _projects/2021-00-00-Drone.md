# The Challenge
I took on this project to push my CAD skills into more geometrically complex territory. Drones have a lot of compound curves and organic surfaces that are difficult to model with standard feature-based tools, making them a good test of surface modelling ability in SolidWorks.

# What I learned
I modelled the full drone in SolidWorks using surface modelling techniques — including lofted surfaces, boundary surfaces, and surface trims — to capture the aerodynamic body geometry. Working through this design significantly improved my comfort with SolidWorks surfacing tools, which I later applied when modelling the crocodile face for the Study Robot Companion project.
